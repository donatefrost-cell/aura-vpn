# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **AURA VPN** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and technical approaches
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for user privacy, security, and reliability

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing, real IPs, VPN keys, credentials) without explicit permission
- Introducing obfuscated code, unverified telemetry, or deceptive behavior into contributions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Report abusive behavior via GitHub Issues or Security Advisories (<https://github.com/donatefrost-cell/aura-vpn>).
